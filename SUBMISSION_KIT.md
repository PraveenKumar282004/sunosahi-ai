## 📝 Part 4: Hackathon Form Submission Answers

When filling out the official submission form on the Gnani portal:

### 1. Project Title:
`SunoSahi AI — Confidence-Aware Multilingual Voice Agent for Service Dispatch`

### 2. The Problem You Are Solving:
`Customer service calls in India are inherently complex: customers switch fluidly between English and Indic languages (code-mixing), speak over loud background noise (traffic, appliances), and frequently use ambiguous references (e.g., "address wohi purana hai" or vague "shaam ko"). Standard voice bots either guess, rely on stale CRM data, or fail entirely—resulting in wasted technician travel, lost revenue, and poor customer experience.`

### 3. Who Will Use It:
`1. Home service platforms, electricians, plumbers, and HVAC maintenance companies who handle high-volume inbound phone calls.
2. Blue-collar field technicians across India who need clear, concise service tickets in their preferred regional language (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi).
3. Indian consumers who want to speak naturally in their mother tongue or Hinglish without being forced into rigid, robotic IVR menus.`

### 4. Gnani AI Models & APIs Used:
`1. Gnani Prisma v3.0 REST STT (api.vachana.ai/stt/v3) — Multilingual Indic speech recognition with auto language detection ('indic-auto'), code-mix formatting, and acoustic clarity scoring.
2. Gnani Timbre v2.5 / v3.0 REST TTS (api.vachana.ai/api/v1/tts/inference) — High-fidelity Indic voice synthesis (Nalini voice) for conversational agent responses and localized technician voice briefings.`

### 5. Each Member's Contribution:
`Solo Entry: Architected and implemented the end-to-end full-stack voice application, integrated Gnani Prisma STT and Timbre TTS APIs, designed the Confidence-Aware Dialogue State Tracking (DST) engine, developed the multilingual technician dispatch UI with Web Audio API waveform visualization, and recorded the public demo.`

### 6. Written Project Explanation:
`SunoSahi AI is a voice agent built on Gnani AI's speech intelligence stack that ensures nothing gets lost in translation. 

The core architectural differentiator of SunoSahi is its 'Confidence-Aware Conversation Protocol'. Unlike traditional voice bots that make binary guesses when encountering ambiguous speech (such as 'wohi purana address' or phonetically ambiguous numbers like 15 vs 50), SunoSahi dynamically computes a triple-metric confidence score: Acoustic Clarity (Prisma SNR), Entity Completeness, and Disambiguation Penalty. 

When confidence falls below threshold, the agent asks a targeted, natural clarification question in conversational Hinglish. Once all entities are validated, SunoSahi automatically synthesizes a structured service ticket and generates a localized technical briefing in the technician's preferred language (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi, English) accompanied by a hands-free voice briefing synthesized via Gnani Timbre TTS.`
